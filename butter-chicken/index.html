Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Delhi's tandoori leftover dish: yogurt-marinated chicken charred hard, then folded into a tomato gravy enriched with cashew, butter and cream.

  • Cuisine: Indian
  • Category: Main
  • Yield: 4 to 6 servings
  • Prep: 30 minutes
  • Cook: 50 minutes
  • Total: 5 hours 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken thigh, in large chunks
  • First marinade: 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • First marinade: 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • First marinade: 1 tsp fine salt
  • Second marinade: 150 g thick full-fat yogurt
  • Second marinade: 15 g ginger paste
  • Second marinade: 15 g garlic paste
  • Second marinade: 1 tsp garam masala
  • Second marinade: half a teaspoon of ground turmeric
  • Second marinade: 1 tbsp mustard oil or ghee
  • Gravy: 800 g ripe tomatoes, quartered
  • Gravy: 50 g raw cashews
  • Gravy: 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • Gravy: 15 g ginger, sliced
  • Gravy: 6 garlic cloves
  • Gravy: 4 green cardamom pods
  • Gravy: 1 small cinnamon stick
  • Gravy: 2 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • Gravy: 100 g unsalted butter, cubed and cold
  • Gravy: 100 ml double cream
  • Gravy: 1 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • Gravy: 1 tsp garam masala
  • Gravy: 1 to 2 tsp sugar, to balance
  • Gravy: fine salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Rub the chicken with lemon juice, chilli powder and salt. Rest 20 minutes. This short acid stage seasons the meat before the yogurt seals it.
  2. Mix the yogurt, ginger, garlic, garam masala, turmeric and oil. Coat the chicken and refrigerate 4 hours, or overnight.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill at maximum heat, or sear in a dry cast iron pan, until blackened in patches and just cooked, about 12 minutes. Do not cook it gently. The char is the whole point of the dish.
  4. Simmer the tomatoes, cashews, onion, ginger, garlic, cardamom, cinnamon and chilli powder with 150 ml water for 25 minutes, until everything is completely soft.
  5. Fish out the cinnamon and cardamom. Blend until smooth, then pass through a fine sieve, pressing the solids. The sieve is what separates restaurant makhani from a rough curry.
  6. Return the gravy to a bare simmer. Whisk in the cold butter a few cubes at a time, never letting it boil, until the sauce turns glossy and pale.
  7. Stir in the cream. Crush the fenugreek leaves between your palms and add them with the garam masala. Season with salt and enough sugar to round off the tomatoes.
  8. Fold in the charred chicken and any resting juices. Simmer 5 minutes so the flavours join, then rest 10 minutes off the heat before serving.

Kasuri methi is not optional

Dried fenugreek leaf is the flavour most people cannot place in restaurant butter chicken. Nothing substitutes for it, and 1 tbsp changes the dish completely.

Crush it between your palms as you add it. Whole leaves stay leathery and taste of hay.

Keeping the sauce from splitting

Cold butter, low heat

The gravy is a butter emulsion, closer to a beurre blanc than to a curry. Boil it after the butter goes in and it breaks into an oily orange slick.

If it does split, pull it off the heat and whisk in 2 tbsp of cold cream to bring it back.
