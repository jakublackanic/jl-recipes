Neapolitan Pizza Margherita

The original three-ingredient pizza: a slow-fermented 62% hydration dough, raw San Marzano tomatoes and fior di latte, baked in about 90 seconds.

  • Cuisine: Italian
  • Category: Main
  • Yield: 4 pizzas of 30 cm
  • Prep: 40 minutes
  • Cook: 2 minutes
  • Total: 26 hours

Ingredients

  • 600 g 00 flour, W260–300 if available
  • 375 g water, cool
  • 18 g fine sea salt
  • 0.6 g instant dry yeast (about a fifth of a teaspoon)
  • 400 g whole peeled San Marzano tomatoes
  • 250 g fior di latte or low-moisture mozzarella
  • 1 handful fresh basil leaves
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Fine sea salt, for the sauce
  • Semolina or fine flour, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Dissolve the salt in the water, then whisk in the yeast. Add about a tenth of the flour and stir to a slurry, which keeps the yeast off the undiluted salt.
  2. Add the remaining flour and mix until no dry patches remain. Rest the shaggy dough, uncovered, for 20 minutes so the flour hydrates fully.
  3. Knead 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and passes a windowpane test. It should feel firm rather than sticky.
  4. Cover and leave at room temperature for 2 hours, then refrigerate for 20–24 hours.
  5. Divide into 4 pieces of about 250 g and shape into tight balls. Cover and prove at room temperature for 4–6 hours, until soft, domed and slack.
  6. Crush the tomatoes by hand, or pass them through a food mill. Season with salt only. Do not cook the sauce and do not blend it, which aerates it pink and makes it watery.
  7. Preheat with a stone or steel at the absolute maximum. A wood or gas pizza oven wants a stone at 430–480 °C. A domestic oven wants a steel under the grill at 250 °C for at least 45 minutes.
  8. Dust a ball with flour and press from the centre outwards, pushing the gas into a 2 cm rim. Never use a rolling pin, which flattens the cornicione. Stretch to 30 cm over your knuckles.
  9. Spread 70–80 g of sauce in a spiral, leaving the rim bare. Add torn mozzarella, a few basil leaves and a thread of olive oil. Work fast so the base does not sweat onto the peel.
  10. Launch onto the stone. In a pizza oven, bake 60–90 seconds, turning every 20 seconds. In a domestic oven, bake 4–6 minutes on the steel, then grill the top for 1–2 minutes to leopard the rim.

Why so little yeast

0.6 g of yeast for 600 g of flour looks like a mistake. It is not. Over a 24 hour cold ferment the yeast multiplies to the population a fast dough starts with, and the slow route builds flavour and an extensible dough that opens without tearing.

Warm kitchen? Cut the yeast to 0.4 g. A dough that overproves collapses on the peel and bakes flat.

Mozzarella management

Keep the water out

Fior di latte holds a lot of whey. Tear it an hour ahead, salt it lightly and drain it in a sieve, or the pizza floods and the centre never crisps.

In a domestic oven the bake is long enough that mozzarella added at the start turns to oil. Add it halfway through instead.
